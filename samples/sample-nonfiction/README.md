# The Quiet Channel

A sample non-fiction book, used as the worked example for the non-fiction
template.

## Status

Sample. Not a real book.

## What to look at

- `chapters/01.md` — per-chapter front matter (Chapter 23.4), with a `status`
  field
- `chapters/02.md` — a shorter chapter, no front matter, to show that it's
  optional
- `notes/sources.md` — the working bibliography
- `notes/figures.md` — figures with a rights column
- `research/` — present and git-ignored

## Build

`./build.sh` or `.\build.ps1`. The DOCX includes a table of contents to depth 2.
