## Chapter 1

It was the kind of morning that made you want to close the curtains.

Marta closed the door behind her and stood for a moment in the hall,
listening to the house settle. The tide was out; she could hear it in the
quality of the quiet, the way the gulls had somewhere to stand.

*She hadn't slept.* **Again.**

> "You're up early," her sister said, from the kitchen doorway.

"I'm up," Marta said. "It isn't the same thing."

***

By noon the snow had stopped, and the road to the village was passable.

They walked down together without speaking, which was the closest they had
come to agreement in eleven years. The inn was at the bottom of the hill,
where it had always been, with its door painted the same green their mother
had chosen and nobody had repainted since.

Ines was already there, which meant she had come the night before and told
nobody. She had a way of arriving early that was not quite a kindness.

"You could have said," Marta said.

"I could have," Ines agreed, and held the door.
