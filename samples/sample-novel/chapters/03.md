## Chapter 3

The funeral was on a Tuesday, which their mother would have hated.

***

Afterwards, in the front room of the inn, with the fire lit because nobody
could think what else to do, Ines said the thing they had all been not saying.

"She wanted to be cremated."

"She didn't," Marta said.

"She told me. In the spring."

"She told me in the spring too, and she told me she wanted the plot next to
Dad, and she told me she didn't care, and she told me to stop fussing." Marta
put her cup down carefully. "She told everyone a different thing. That was
the whole of her."

***

Later, when the others had gone and the inn was quiet, Marta went back to the
drawer.

The box was where she had left it. The papers were where she had left them.
The letter was gone.

She stood for a long moment with her hand in the empty space, and then she
closed the drawer, and went to find her sister.
