# Short (50 words)

Marthe Dubois is the author of *The Salt House*. She lives on the coast she
writes about, which she says is either research or an excuse.

# Long (200 words)

Marthe Dubois writes literary fiction about families, coastlines, and the
things people don't say. *The Salt House* is her first novel.

She has worked as a bookseller, a copyeditor, and — briefly and badly — a
fisherman. She lives on the coast she writes about, in a house that is not on
a cliff, with a dog who is not in any of her books.
