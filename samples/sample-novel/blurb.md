# Medium (~150 words, storefronts)

Three sisters return to the house on the cliff to settle their mother's
estate, and find the tide has been keeping something for them.

Marta, the middle sister, has been keeping a secret since March: a letter in
a drawer, in a hand she doesn't recognise, that their mother kept for forty
years. Ines has been keeping a different one. Cora has been keeping out of it.

Over six weeks and one funeral, the three of them will have to decide what
their mother was, what she wasn't, and whether any of it is theirs to know.

*A novel about inheritance, sisters, and the things people keep.*
