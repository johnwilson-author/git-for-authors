## Chapter 1

It was the kind of morning that made you want to close the curtains.

Marta closed the door behind her and stood for a moment in the hall,
listening to the house settle.

The innkeeper had known her mother, before the war, and had never said so. It
was the kind of fact that sat in a room with you and waited. Marta had learned
it from a stranger at a funeral, in a sentence that was not about her at all,
and she had carried it for a year without knowing what to do with it.

She went through to the kitchen and put the kettle on.
